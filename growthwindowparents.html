Questions parents ask

Good questions

How accurate is the prediction?

We use the Khamis-Roche method, built from a long-term study of children followed into adulthood. In that study, 9 in 10 predictions fell within about 5 cm of actual adult height. That's why every report shows a range, not a single number.

What ages does it work for?

Children aged 4 to 17. It's most useful from around 8 to 15, when the gap between early and late developers is biggest.

Is this what academies do?

Professional academies use growth and maturity measures like these to "bio-band" players, grouping them by physical development as well as age. The Premier League has used the same Khamis-Roche method in Growth Window to bio-band academy players aged 12 to 15, and Scottish academies screen for it too. Growth Window brings the same idea to parents outside an academy. We're not affiliated with any club or governing body.

What happens to my child's data?

We use the measurements only to make and send your report, never for marketing, and we delete them 30 days after sending. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.
