Recover

An 8-month-old kitchen quote, back from the dead

Sarah asked Clyde Kitchens for a quote back in January. Nobody followed up after the first call, and the enquiry went cold. Recover reaches out on its own, months later, at exactly the moment she's ready to talk again.

  • 1Recover texts every dormant lead in the uploaded list, spaced out naturally rather than all at once.
  • 2A reply comes in - Recover reads it, decides it's a hot lead, and alerts Craig instantly.
  • 3Craig takes it from there on his own phone. Recover's job is done the moment a human's needed.

Recalls

A happy customer, fourteen months on

James had a new kitchen fitted by Clyde Kitchens last year. He was never going to think to ask about a bathroom on his own - so Recalls does the asking, at a moment tied to a real reason to reach out.

  • 1Recalls runs against your own past-customer list, not a stranger's - same engine as Recover, aimed differently.
  • 2The message is built around a real offer you choose - a renewal, a check-in, a natural upsell.
  • 3Set up together with you on a call rather than self-serve, since the offer changes every time.

Speed

A Sunday-evening enquiry, answered in under a minute

Tom submits a website enquiry about a leaning tree at 9:14pm on a Sunday. Nobody's near a phone. Speed replies before he's closed the tab - and the appointment's booked before Liam even sees it come through.

  • 1Speed connects to your website form or Facebook/Google lead ads - a reply goes out in under 60 seconds, any hour.
  • 2It asks the one or two questions that tell you whether it's worth a call - urgency, timing, budget.
  • 3A qualified reply gets handed straight to your phone. Liam wakes up to a booking already in the diary.
