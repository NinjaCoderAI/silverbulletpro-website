Fair questions

Does it replace me or talk to my customers like a robot?

It sends a short text the moment a call goes unanswered, asks a few questions you choose, and hands anything urgent, awkward or about price to you. It never quotes a price and never promises a time it cannot see.

How do I get missed calls to it?

After you sign up and pay, we email your new number and the one-time call-forwarding steps for your phone. Calls you do not answer forward to that number, and the text goes out automatically.

What if the caller has an emergency?

If their reply sounds urgent, INSTANT drops the script, tells them you are getting someone to call, and alerts you straight away so you can ring them.

Can it book jobs into my diary?

Yes, if you connect a Cal.com calendar. It offers real free slots and books one. If you do not connect a calendar, it collects their details and hands the lead to you to book.

Is there a contract?

No. It is a flat monthly price and you can cancel any time. If it is not earning its keep, stop paying for it.
