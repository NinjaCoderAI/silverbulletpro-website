Reasonable Questions

Before you ask — we probably already have.

Will it sound like a robot?

No. It uses your business name, your words and the questions you'd actually ask. Most people reply to it exactly as they would to you, and every message is visible to you as it happens.

What if someone asks something it can't handle?

It stops and flags the conversation for you rather than guessing — straight to your WhatsApp, SMS or email, whichever you've set up. You take it from there, in the same thread.

Is texting people like this legal in the UK?

Yes. Recover and Recalls only ever contact people who've previously enquired or bought from you, and Speed only replies to someone who's just submitted an enquiry to you. Every message carries an opt-out, and nobody is ever cold-texted.

Do I need to change my CRM or website?

No. Recover and Recalls just need a CSV export of your list — any format works. Speed connects to your existing website form or Facebook/Google lead ads through a quick integration we set up for you.

What's the actual difference between Recover and Recalls?

Same engine, different list. Recover reaches out to old, dead leads and quotes that never closed. Recalls reaches out to people who've already bought from you, for renewals, service reminders or upsells. Recalls is set up together with you rather than self-serve.

How quickly can I be live?

Recover and Recalls: as soon as your list is uploaded, usually the same day. Speed: within 24 hours of a short setup call.

What happens to my leads or customers if I cancel?

They're yours. Every conversation is exported to you whether you continue or not.
