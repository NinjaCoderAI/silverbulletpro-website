Right after you pay, your INSTANT number and the one-time call-forwarding steps appear on screen and in your email. Calls you do not answer forward to that number, and the text goes out automatically. The setup page ticks green when your first missed call has been texted back.

What if it is an emergency?

If their reply sounds urgent, INSTANT drops the script, tells them you are getting someone to call, and alerts you straight away so you can ring them. If someone's life is at risk, it tells them to call 999.

What if they ring from a landline or hide their number?

Landlines and withheld numbers cannot receive texts, so there is nothing to send. Mobile callers, which is most enquiries, are covered.

Can it book jobs into my diary?

Yes, if you connect a Cal.com calendar. It offers real free slots and books one. Without a calendar it collects their details and hands the lead to you to book.
