Reasonable questions

Will it sound like a robot?

It uses your words, your business name and the questions you would ask yourself. Most people reply to it exactly as they would reply to you, and you can read every message as it happens.

What if someone asks something odd?

If a message goes outside what SPEED can handle, it stops and alerts you rather than guessing. You take over from your own phone, in the same thread.

Do I need to change my website or CRM?

No. The simplest setup is connecting your website form or your Facebook/Google lead ads through a quick integration we set up for you. Nothing changes about how leads reach you today — SPEED just gets a copy of each one, instantly.

Is texting leads legal in the UK?

Yes, where the person has submitted an enquiry to you and is expecting a response. SPEED only ever replies to inbound enquiries and never cold texts. Every message carries an opt-out.

What happens to my leads if I cancel?

They are yours. Every conversation is exported to you whether you continue or not.

How quickly can I be live?

Within 24 hours of your setup call, usually the same day.

Do you offer a discount to get started?

Sometimes — if you've been given a code, enter it at checkout and it'll come off your first payment.
